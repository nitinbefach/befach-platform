What are the import duties on cotton textiles (HS 5208) from India to the United States?
Befach AI
Cotton textile imports (HS 5208) — India to US:

• Base MFN duty: 8.4% - 12.0% ad valorem (varies by weave type)
• Merchandise Processing Fee: 0.3464% (min $27.75, max $538.40)
• Harbor Maintenance Fee: 0.125% (sea freight only)

India lost GSP eligibility in 2019, so no preferential rates apply. Consider sourcing from countries with active FTAs for better duty treatment.
