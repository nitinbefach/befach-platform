# Homepage Reference Design

## Overview
These reference images were used to redesign the Befach International homepage on November 27, 2025.

## Design Elements Implemented

### 1. Our Services Section
- Main banner with "End to End Process Handling"
- 4 service cards: Sourcing, Logistics, EX-IM Data, Clearance
- Brown gradient header card
- Circular icons for each service

### 2. Top Categories Section
- 4 main categories with circular bordered icons: Electronics, Fashion, Skin Care, Accessories
- "Other Categories" grid with smaller icons
- "View All" link

### 3. Featured Products Section
- Product cards with image, name, min order, supplier, rating, price
- "Buy Now" button on each card
- 4-column grid layout

### 4. Our Partners Section
- Partner logos in a horizontal row
- Meta, Compact Disc, WiFi, PF logos

### 5. Special Offer Banner
- Orange background
- "Offer Valid for First 100 Customers"
- Bullet points with checkmarks
- "Shop Now" button
- Image on the right

### 6. News & Blogs Section
- 2-column grid of blog cards
- Image thumbnail on left
- Title, excerpt, and "Read More" link

### 7. FAQ Section
- Accordion-style expandable questions
- Orange question text
- Plus/minus toggle icons
- FAQ illustration on the right

### 8. Clients Testimonials Section
- Testimonial cards with user photo
- Quote icon overlay
- Name, role, testimonial text
- Dot navigation for slider

### 9. Footer
- Logo and social links on left
- Navigation links in center
- Newsletter subscription form on right
- Copyright at bottom

## Color Palette
- Primary Orange: #F57C00
- Brown: #8B5A2B
- Background Cream: #FFF5E6, #FFF9F0
- Text Dark: #333
- Text Gray: #666, #888

## Typography
- Section headings: Bold, centered
- Card titles: Semi-bold
- Body text: Regular weight
